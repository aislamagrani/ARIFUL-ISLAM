About

I work in the Industrial Credit Division of a state-owned commercial bank in Bangladesh, where I appraise industrial project loan proposals from start to finish. That covers technical, financial and marketing viability, credit memo preparation and credit valuation.

My financial feasibility work covers net present value (NPV), internal rate of return (IRR), payback period, profitability index, benefit-cost ratio (BCR), cash flow projections, return on equity, return on investment and loan amortization. I have applied these to spinning, dyeing and printing, weaving and knitting, and garments projects.

I work within Bangladesh Bank guidelines, loan classification standards and CIB data requirements, so that each recommendation is sound for both the borrower and the bank.

My textile engineering training in wet processing lets me test a project’s machinery, process flow, capacity and raw material assumptions before the numbers are modelled. That is where a technical view adds the most to credit appraisal.
